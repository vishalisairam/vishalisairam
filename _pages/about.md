## Professional Background

I am a Data Scientist and Policy Analyst with a unique blend of technical expertise and deep understanding of public policy. Currently working as a Consultant with the **World Bank's Poverty and Equity Team**, I specialize in creating data-driven solutions that address complex development challenges.

My work spans multiple domains including:
- **Data Science & Analytics**: Advanced statistical analysis, machine learning, and causal inference methods
- **Policy Analysis**: Quantitative evaluation of welfare schemes and development interventions
- **Technology for Development**: Creating solutions that bridge the digital divide
- **GIS & Spatial Analysis**: Using satellite data and administrative datasets for research
