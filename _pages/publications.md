## Peer-Reviewed Publications

### BlendNet: An Assisted Digital Distribution Platform for Underserved Population
**Authors:** **Vishali Sairam**, Apurv Mehra, Kashish Mittal  
**Venue:** ACM SIGCAS/SIGCHI Conference on Computing and Sustainable Societies (COMPASS), 2024  
**Status:** Published

**Abstract:**  
This paper presents BlendNet, a novel cloud-edge-satellite platform designed to serve underserved populations through assisted digital distribution. Based on pilot deployment data from ~68,000 users and 250 retailers, we analyze the effectiveness of technology-mediated service delivery in resource-constrained environments.

**Key Contributions:**
- First large-scale analysis of hybrid cloud-edge-satellite deployment in underserved communities
- Empirical evaluation of user adoption patterns and service utilization
- Framework for designing technology solutions for digital divide contexts
- Policy recommendations for scaling digital distribution platforms

**Research Impact:** This work contributes to the growing body of literature on ICT4D (Information and Communication Technologies for Development) and provides practical insights for organizations working to bridge the digital divide.

### Economic Insecurity Increases Polarization and Decreases Trust
**Authors:** **Vishali Sairam**, Vincent Heddesheimer, Joanna Bryson  
**Year:** 2022  
**Venues:** 
- European Political Science Association Conference 2022, Prague
- International Conference on Social Dilemmas 2022, Copenhagen

**Abstract:**  
Using experimental and survey data, this research examines the relationship between economic insecurity and social trust. Our findings demonstrate that economic uncertainty systematically increases political polarization while decreasing interpersonal and institutional trust.

**Key Findings:**
- Economic insecurity leads to measurable increases in political polarization
- Trust in institutions declines significantly under conditions of economic uncertainty  
- Effects are consistent across different demographic groups and countries
- Policy implications for social cohesion and democratic stability

**Methodology:**
- Behavioral economics experiments with 1,200+ participants
- Cross-national survey data analysis
- Causal identification using instrumental variables approach
- Robustness checks across multiple model specifications

**Policy Relevance:** This research provides evidence-based insights for policymakers addressing rising polarization and declining trust in democratic societies.
